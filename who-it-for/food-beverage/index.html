The Problem

Why Do 80% of Diners Never Come Back?

They loved the food. But you have zero way to reach them again.

01

Walk-In = Zero Data

A guest walks in, has an incredible meal, pays — and walks out as a complete stranger. No email. No phone number. No way to ever reach them again. That's not a customer, that's a ghost.

02

Discount Addiction

Groupon, Yelp Deals, 50% off. You train guests to come only for deals — they vanish when the discount ends.

03

No Post-Visit Engagement

Check paid. Then… nothing. No follow-up, no "come back soon," no reason to think of you next Friday night.

04

Delivery App Dependency

UberEats, DoorDash, Grubhub — 30% of every order. Your food, your kitchen, your staff… their customer.

05

Seasonal Revenue Dips

January empties. Summer clears the room. No system to pull guests back when foot traffic drops.

06

Can't Identify Regulars

Your best guest has been in 12 times. Your host doesn't recognize them. No preferences saved. No special treatment. Just another table number. That's not hospitality — that's amnesia.

You're spending more acquiring new diners than keeping the ones who already loved you.
