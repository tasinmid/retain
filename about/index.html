How We Started

How Retain Actually Started

Retain started in 2020, born out of a frustration we all felt as customers. We were tired of businesses forgetting who we were, repeating ourselves to different support agents, and receiving generic messages that clearly weren't meant for us.

What started as a simple idea—what if businesses could actually remember their customers?—quickly grew into something much bigger. We realized that the problem wasn't just about data storage; it was about building genuine relationships at scale.

We built Retain to solve one thing really well: helping businesses remember their customers so they can deliver personalized experiences that make people feel valued. No complicated setups, no overwhelming features—just a simple, powerful tool to help you stay connected with the customers you've worked so hard to earn.

Today, Retain helps hundreds of businesses across industries turn one-time buyers into loyal customers. We're not here to chase trends or add unnecessary complexity. We're here to help you build the kind of relationships that keep people coming back.
